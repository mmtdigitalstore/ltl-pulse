## 3. Logo recommendation (brief for designer)

**Not the same as the site favicon** (pulse waveform) unless you deliberately unify later. Channel art needs to read at tiny sizes and on mobile.

### Deliverables to request
1. **Channel icon** (square) — 800×800 px min (YouTube uses round crop)
2. **Banner / cover** — 2560×1440 px (safe area ~1546×423 centered)
3. Optional: **watermark** for video corner (simple mark)

### Visual direction
- **Background:** charcoal / near-black `#151518` (match site), *not* flat navy wash
- **Accent:** gold `#FFB400`
- **Optional depth:** subtle navy `#0B2072` only as a thin rule or soft glow — don’t make the icon “all blue”
- **Wordmark:** “LTL” dominant; “Conversations” secondary *or* a strong monogram **LC** / pulse + L
- **Typography:** elegant serif or refined display for LTL (site uses Playfair-like heading energy); clean sans for subtitle
- **Avoid:** generic purple gradients, stock “mic in circle,” cluttered taglines on the icon

### Concept options (pick one for designer)
| Concept | Idea |
|---------|------|
| A — Wordmark | “LTL” in gold on charcoal; thin “CONVERSATIONS” under |
| B — Monogram | Interlocking L + pulse tick (from favicon) in gold |
| C — Dual brand | Small “LTL Pulse” lockup + “Conversations” as show title on banner only |

**Banner copy suggestion:** `LTL Conversations` · `Where leadership meets culture` · `Season 1` · `ltlpulse.com`

## 4. What intro video should live on the channel

**Type:** Channel trailer / “start here” video (60–90 seconds).  
**Not:** a full episode. **Not:** a hard sales webinar.

### Purpose
1. Who this is for (owners, managers, coaches in service businesses)
2. What they’ll get (practical, culture + leadership, future-ready)
3. How Season 1 works (free weekly conversations)
4. Where to go deeper (ltlpulse.com — Pulse, Cadence, experts)
5. Soft CTA: Subscribe on YouTube + visit /podcast for the hub

### Suggested structure (timecode)
| Time | Content |
|------|---------|
| 0:00–0:08 | Hook — “If you’re growing a service business and leadership still feels like hustle…” |
| 0:08–0:25 | Promise — conversations that help you keep people, win customers, build systems |
| 0:25–0:45 | Faces — quick cuts of Dawn / Jackie / Sylvan / Joshua (or voice-over + stills if video not ready) |
| 0:45–1:05 | How to watch — Season 1 weekly · free on YouTube & LTL Pulse |
| 1:05–1:20 | CTA — Subscribe · ltlpulse.com/podcast · “Where leadership meets culture” |

### Tone
Warm, credible, problem-led — not corporate hype. Same voice as the site.

### Music
Custom or licensed bed: modern, warm, light pulse/rhythm; no generic “tech startup” EDM. Designer/audio person delivers 15s loop + 90s bed + outro sting.

### Also upload early
- Episode 1 (when ready) as first “real” content  
- Optional: 30s “Season 1 is coming” teaser if trailer isn’t ready

## 5. HeyGen vs Fiverr for the intro

| | **HeyGen (AI avatar)** | **Fiverr (human creator)** |
|--|------------------------|----------------------------|
| Best for | Fast draft, placeholder, testing script | Polished brand trailer people remember |
| Risk | Can feel generic / “AI host” vs real LTL experts | Quality varies — vet portfolios |
| Fit for LTL | Weak as the *permanent* channel face (trust + leadership brand) | Strong if you brief well |
| Cost | Subscription | One-off gig |

**Recommendation:**  
- **Do not** make HeyGen the permanent channel intro if the show is about real experts and culture.  
- **Optional:** use HeyGen only as a **scratch animatic** to lock timing/script for the designer.  
- **Hire on Fiverr (or similar)** for: motion designer + editor who can use your real footage/stills + custom music.  
- **Best case:** Dawn (or team) on camera 20–30 seconds + motion graphics + music — highest trust.

**Fiverr search terms:** “YouTube channel intro trailer,” “podcast trailer motion graphics,” “YouTube banner and logo kit.”  
Ask for: logo variants, banner, 60–90s trailer, music bed, end card with URL.
