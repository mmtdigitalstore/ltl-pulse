## 5. Audio / tone notes

- **Music:** Low, confident, modern — not corporate elevator, not hype EDM. Think documentary / leadership journal.
- **Pacing:** Trailer = cinematic pauses. Episode teaser = tighter, one idea per frame.
- **No countdown clocks** on unlock dates — show the date only (site matches this tone).
- **End card:** Always gold CTA on dark; never white background end cards.
